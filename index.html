Trust, verified

Don't take our word for it — check the record

OneQAZ doesn't sell buy/sell calls. It publishes timestamped judgments and calibrated probabilities — hits and misses alike — in a record that can't be quietly rewritten afterwards.
Prediction Ledger→
A daily SHA-256 hash chain over every created and resolved prediction, with the recipe published so anyone can recompute it. Hash Chain
Ledger Anchors→
Public anchors of the hash chain, timestamped to the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps. OpenTimestamps
All-time Record→
Every closed paper trade since public attestation began — win rate, win/loss ratio and profit factor, losing stretches included. Paper Trading
Methodology→
How fills, fees and reporting windows are modeled — including the assumptions that flatter the numbers. Assumptions
Corrections→
Defects we found in our own record, and how they were fixed — disclosed in public. Disclosure
AI agents can run the same checks over MCP: get_ledger_integrity get_resolved_predictions get_prediction_accuracy

For AI agents

Connect once, then just ask

OneQAZ is a remote MCP server. Add it once to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code — no API key on the free tier (1,500 calls/day) — and ask in plain language. Every answer carries a research-only disclaimer.
“How’s the market today?”Regime, the strongest signals and yesterday’s paper-trading outcomes in one call.get_daily_brief
“What does OneQAZ make of BTC?”The research signal and score for a symbol, and why the system decided what it did.get_signals explain_decision
“Is the macro backdrop risk-on or risk-off?”Eight macro categories and the macro → market hypotheses, with their lags.market://global/summary get_macro_influence_map
“What are you holding, and how did it go?”Open paper positions, closed trades, drawdown and Sharpe — losses included, before fees.get_positions get_trade_history get_performance_metrics
“How accurate are your predictions?”Hit rates against persistence and majority-class baselines, and whether stated confidence matches reality.get_prediction_accuracy get_signal_calibration
“How do I know it wasn’t edited later?”A daily SHA-256 chain over the prediction ledger, plus the raw rows to recompute it yourself.get_ledger_integrity get_resolved_predictions
